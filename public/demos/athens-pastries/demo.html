Our story

A Greektown staple.

For more than four decades, Athens Pastries has been the stop on the Danforth for a warm spanakopita and a coffee on the patio. The pies are made the same way they always were: hand-layered phyllo, real feta, nothing frozen in between. Founding year and family story to be filled in with the owners.

Light, flaky phyllo and loukoumades worth the walk.

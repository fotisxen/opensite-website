Our story

From Greece at 18 to the Danforth.

Louis Kontos came to Toronto from Greece at eighteen and opened Mister Greek Meats on the Danforth in 1972. His children Bill and Nick grew up behind the counter. Today the shop supplies Greektown families, supermarkets and restaurants across Canada, with the same cuts and the same care.

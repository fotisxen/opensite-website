Legal & corporate

Legal matters in Greece, with lawyers who speak your language.

Divorce or change of name recognised in Greece, a company in Athens, a Golden Visa, a marriage on an island or a birth registered in your municipality. Our Athens legal team and partner notaries take care of it.

With six lawyers in Athens and partner attorneys and notaries across Greece, we represent you before courts, registries and ministries, and we keep you updated in English or French at every step.

Greece is moving to simplify citizenship for third- and fourth-generation Greeks abroad. If your grandparents were Greek, ask us where your family registration stands.

What we do

  • Recognition of foreign court decisions (divorce, adoption, change of name)
  • Registration of birth, marriage and death
  • Opening a family registration (merida)
  • Getting married in Greece
  • Greek citizenship for the diaspora
  • Incorporating a company in Greece
  • Representing business interests
  • Golden Visa, hospitality & tourism investments

How it works

  1. ConsultationWith a lawyer, by video or at the office.
  2. DocumentsApostille and certified translation, done by us.
  3. Filing & follow-upWe file and follow up with the court or registry.

Documents you’ll usually need

Questions we hear often

My Canadian divorce is not shown in Greece. Why?

Foreign decisions have to be recognised and registered in Greece. Until then, Greek records still show you as married, which can block an inheritance or a sale.

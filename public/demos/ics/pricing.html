How do annual plans work?

You pay once a year and we take care of everything in the plan for 12 months. 30 days before renewal we email you; you can switch off auto-renew any time in your portal.

Which currencies and cards?

CAD, USD and EUR. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay, processed securely by Stripe. ICS never sees your card number.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes, by email right away, and all your invoices stay in your client portal.

What if a service turns out not to be needed?

If we have not started the work, we refund you in full.

Property in Greece

Your property in Greece, looked after while you are away.

ICS is the largest North American firm managing property in Greece. We find tenants, collect rent, pay the bills, keep the house in good shape and, when you are ready, sell it for the right price.

Owning property in Greece from abroad is hard: finding a good tenant, collecting rent, paying bills on time, repairs, trespassers, and a new rule every year. Our team in Athens, with lawyers, accountants and engineers, takes all of it on, and you see everything in your client portal.

Since April 2025, cleaning plots of land is mandatory for fire prevention, with fines for owners who do not. Our Property Care plan arranges it every year.

What we do

  • Buying, selling and leasing
  • Property valuation
  • Finding and screening tenants
  • Rent collection and bill payment
  • Repairs, maintenance and renovation
  • Legalisation of disputed properties
  • Engineer and energy certificates
  • Representation before utilities and authorities

How it works

  1. ValuationOur real estate consultant visits and gives you a written valuation.
  2. PaperworkEngineer certificate, energy certificate, cadastre, tax clearance.
  3. Listed on our portalWith photos, video and viewings, on propertiesforsaleingreece.com.
  4. ContractSigned with a power of attorney, no need to travel.

Documents you’ll usually need

📎 Title deed📎 E9📎 Cadastre extract📎 Power of attorney

Questions we hear often

How much does full management cost?

For rented property we charge a percentage of the rent, so we earn when you earn. For empty homes or land, the Property Care plan is a fixed yearly fee.

Can I sell without coming to Greece?

Yes. You sign a power of attorney here and we handle viewings, negotiation and the contract.

Where can I see the properties you sell?

On our real estate site, ICS Real Estate, with every listing in Athens, the Peloponnese and the islands.

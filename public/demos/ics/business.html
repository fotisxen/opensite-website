Business: localization, PR & trade

Bring your business to the Greek market.

Twelve million people live in Greece and Cyprus, and over seven million Greeks live abroad. We adapt your products and messages to them, and represent your company in Greece or in Canada.

Localization goes beyond translation: language as people really speak it, symbols, colours, payment habits and legal requirements. We localize websites, ads, brochures, manuals and press releases, plan events and media campaigns for the Greek community, and open doors with Greek and Canadian authorities and industry.

What we do

  • Website and app localization
  • Ads, brochures, catalogues, newsletters
  • Marketing plans and product launches
  • Trade shows, events and media presentations
  • Press releases and speeches
  • A representative office in Greece or Canada
  • Sales and business development
  • Market reports and company background checks

Immigration to Canada

Make Canada your new home.

With Tseliou Immigration Consulting Services (TICS), licensed immigration consultants, we handle work and study permits, sponsorship and citizenship, and help you settle in Toronto or Montreal.

Young Greeks aged 18–35 can work in Canada for up to two years through the International Youth Mobility programme. We check your eligibility, prepare the application, translate your documents and help with your first steps when you land.

What we do

  • International Youth Mobility (work permit 18–35)
  • Work permits and LMIA
  • Study permits
  • Visitor visas
  • Provincial nominee programmes
  • Federal skilled trades
  • Spousal sponsorship
  • Citizenship applications and PR card renewals

How it works

  1. Eligibility checkAge, studies, profession, family.
  2. ApplicationPrepared and filed by our licensed partners.
  3. Arrival supportTranslations, SIN, bank and first steps.

Documents you’ll usually need

📎 Passport📎 Degrees📎 CV📎 Police certificate

Questions we hear often

I am 29 and live in Greece. Can I work in Canada?

Very likely, through the youth mobility programme for ages 18–35. Places are limited each year, so start early.

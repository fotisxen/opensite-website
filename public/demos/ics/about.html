Our story

Born from the needs
of Greeks abroad.

ICS grew out of Anastasios Moussas’ long experience serving the Greek community of Canada, and from the need to help people worn out by trying to settle their affairs in Greece from a distance. It started with certified translations and grew into a full firm, with lawyers, notaries, tax specialists, translators and engineers, working with North American standards of service.

Today we have offices in Toronto, Montreal (Laval), New York, Athens, Thessaloniki and Nicosia, and we are planning Vancouver and Chicago.

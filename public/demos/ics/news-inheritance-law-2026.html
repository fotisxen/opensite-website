New inheritance law: what changes for wills, property, spouses and debts

ELEleonora Lekka · Real Estate Consultant, Athens

Law 5303/2026 (Government Gazette A’ 81/22 May 2026) replaces the fifth book of the Greek Civil Code. Its main rules apply to estates of people who die on or after 16 September 2026, with transitional rules for older wills and pending cases.

Forced heirship becomes a money claim

Until now, close relatives protected by forced heirship became co-owners of specific assets, so a house left to one child could end up divided among several heirs, unused and impossible to sell. Under the new law they keep their financial protection, but as a personal money claim, not a share of the property.

Wills, age 16 and inheritance contracts

  • Handwritten (holographic) wills stay valid, with stronger checks on authenticity.
  • People can make a will from the age of 16.
  • From 16 September 2026, inheritance contracts signed before a notary can arrange the estate in advance, useful for family businesses.

Spouses and partners

Without a will, the surviving spouse now receives a larger share in some cases, notably when there is only one child, and cohabiting partners gain new protection.

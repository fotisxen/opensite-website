Translation & interpretation

Certified translations, accepted on both sides of the Atlantic.

Greek, English and French, translated by accredited in-house translators into their mother tongue and proof-read by a second specialist. Most certificates are ready in one business day.

We are accredited by ATIO (Ontario), OTTIAQ (Québec), CTTIC and ATA, as well as PEM-FIT and PEEMPIP in Greece, and we are a registered supplier of the Translation Bureau of Public Services and Procurement Canada. Our translations can be certified for use in Canada, the USA and Greece, and we can arrange notarisation and apostille.

Only your project manager and your translator see your documents. If a spelling or terminology error ever slips through, we correct it at once, at no cost.

Questions we hear often

How much does a certified translation cost?

Usually CA$25–50 per page, or CA$0.16–0.24 per word for longer texts, depending on difficulty, volume and deadline. Use the instant quote to see your price.

Will the Greek consulate accept it?

Yes. Our translations are certifiable by all Hellenic consular and diplomatic missions in North America, and by Canadian, US and Greek authorities.

Which file formats do you accept?

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images and more. You can also bring or mail the printed originals to any office.

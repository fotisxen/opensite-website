Our village story

It started at my parents' taverna.

I first stepped into hospitality as a child, helping at my parents' taverna in our village in Greece. I've spent my life in restaurants since. Horio is that village taverna, here on the Danforth.

The food begins with my mom. Every recipe carries a lifetime in the kitchen, and we don't believe in shortcuts. With it: cocktails built on Greek herbs and spirits, and Greek wines chosen to sit next to the food.

"Greek food is not about perfection. Greek food is a celebration of life."
